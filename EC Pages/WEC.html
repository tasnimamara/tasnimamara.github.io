Waterloo Engineering Competition

Overview

Annual design competition where engineering students tackle real‑world problems under time constraints. For Junior Design, my team of four had 8 h to design, build, and present a functional prototype.

The Challenge

Build a floating rescue vehicle to deliver aid between two communities at different elevations, navigating obstacles and currents. Budget: $2000 (simulated).

Key Requirements

  • Max size 13 × 13 cm · must float & navigate mechanically
  • Deliver payload at two stations with different heights
  • Materials within budget · 30‑point bonus for zero post‑launch contact

Our Solution

Dual‑propulsion boat: (1) balloon‑powered air thrust for initial speed, (2) elastic‑band‑driven paddle wheel for sustained movement. Elevated deck with side ramps released aid via tilt. Design inspired by balloon cars and rubber‑band boats (see below).

Process & Outcome

8‑hour sprint: Measured water tank, brainstormed concepts, iterated on prototypes. Achieved floatation and basic movement, but second propulsion stage remained unrealised due to time. Didn’t place, but gained intense experience in rapid prototyping, teamwork, and design under pressure.
