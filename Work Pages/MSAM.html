Project 1: Advanced Sensor Component Development

Timeline: Ongoing | Role: Undergraduate Research Assistant

Responsibilities & Contributions

  • Nickel Plating (Electroplating): Applying it to enhance surface stability and prevent unwanted electrical contact between layers at elevated temperatures, ensuring reliable sensor performance.
  • Thin-Film Deposition & Etching: Learning fabrication of specialized sensor components using precision techniques including operation of Astrojet systems for controlled material application.
  • Material Testing & Analysis: Performed laboratory analysis and systematic data collection to evaluate material performance under simulated stress environments, contributing to reliability assessments.
  • Composite Formulation: Shadowing PhD researchers in developing thermally stable composite formulations capable of withstanding extreme operating conditions required for advanced sensor applications.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Hands-on experience with industrial-scale fabrication equipment
  • Understanding of material behavior under stress conditions
  • Exposure to quality control and testing protocols
  • Collaboration with senior materials scientists on R&D projects

Project 2: 3D Printable Biocompatible Silicone for Hearing Aids

Timeline: Ongoing | Role: Research Analyst

Project Background

The lab is researching methods to 3D print biocompatible silicone with customizable color options for hearing aid applications. This project aims to change hearing aid manufacturing by enabling personalized, comfortable, and aesthetically pleasing devices.

Deliverable 1: Literature Review

Task: Comprehensive analysis of 3D printing silicone technologies, processes, advantages, limitations, and commercial suppliers.

Note: This deliverable was posted with permission from employers.

Literature Review Key Findings:

  • Analysis of various 3D printing technologies compatible with silicone materials
  • Review of current market suppliers and material capabilities
  • Identification of technical challenges and potential solutions

Deliverable 2: Printer & Supplier Analysis

Task: Identify top 5 printers and suppliers of biocompatible silicone, analyze pros/cons of each, and prepare presentation for stakeholder company.

Note: This deliverable was posted with permission from employers.

Analysis Criteria:

  • Print resolution and accuracy for medical-grade applications
  • Material biocompatibility certification and safety standards
  • Color customization capabilities and limitations
  • Cost-effectiveness for production-scale manufacturing
  • Technical support and supplier reliability
  • Post-processing requirements and complexity

Note: This analysis is currently in progress. The completed presentation will be added to this page by Febuary.
