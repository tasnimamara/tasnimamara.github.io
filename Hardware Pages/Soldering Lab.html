Soldering Lab Experience

Soldering is the process of joining two or more electronic components together by melting solder around the connection. It is a fundamental skill in electronics and is essential for building and repairing circuits. Thankfully, I built that skill during my FireFighter Bot project and this lab helped reinforce it. Here's some pictures from my lab in which I built a fully functioning circuit with a switch and 2 LEDs.
