Triboelectric Nanogenerator

Day 1 – concept & CAD

Designed TENG for campers needing light on windy nights. Final concept: aluminium ball inside 3D‑printed vortex whistle rolls between wool and teflon coated walls, generating charge via contact electrification. Started SolidWorks model.

Day 3 & 4 – print & assembly

Operated Raise3D E2 printer. Assembled: glued aluminium sheets to inner walls, added wool (pos) and teflon (neg) with copper wire contacts. Hammered aluminium foil into balls. Final test yielded 0.4 V – proof of concept.
