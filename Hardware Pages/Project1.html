firefighter bot

project overview

autonomous robot that navigates a maze to detect and extinguish a candle flame.

core components

PIC16f18875 microcontroller - x3 PCBs (Traxmaker Pro) - x2 ultrasonic - IR flame sensors - LCD - 12V fan - threaded lamp rod chassis

key skills

PCB design & fabrication - soldering - C (GCBasic) - sensor integration - autonomous navigation - circuit troubleshooting - mechanical assembly - team leadership

foundation

completed 10 breadboard assignments (logic gates, binary clocks). learned schematic reading, multimeter debugging, short/bad connection diagnosis. drafted project proposal with sketch, BOM, and timeline (approved, 99/100).

system integration

maze navigation & flameout

coded GCBasic algorithm: wall following + flame search. iterative testing – adjusted IR thresholds. final run: robot navigates maze, detects candle, activates fan -> flame extinguished.
