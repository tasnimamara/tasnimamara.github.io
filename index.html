What is Nanotechnology Engineering?

Nanotechnology engineering is a blend of electrical engineering, chemical engineering, and mechanical/mechatronics/materials engineering (a truly interdisiplenary field XD). At its core though, nanotechnology engineering is about solving big problems by working at the smallest scale. Nano engineers bring a new way of problem solving and act as a bridge between the nanoscale and real world innovations. Whether you need stronger, lighter materials for mechanical systems, faster and more efficient electronic components, smarter chemical processes, or entirely new materials designed from the ground up, my skills will deliver that next-generation advantage.
